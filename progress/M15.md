# M15

Status: complete, smoke tested on Linux with the real binaries
Started: 2026-09-14

## Goal

Relay hygiene. Close the relay side of what M14 left open and two backlog
rows from M7: a delisted author's records leave on the next sweep unless
a pin holds them, blobs nothing references any more are collected, and a
refused paid push leaves no dead receipt in the publisher's store. The
demo: an operator delists an author and sends SIGHUP; the author's free
pages and manifest are gone, the paid ones stay until their pin passes,
and a minute later their blobs are gone from disk; a publisher whose
receipt is refused runs `weft receipts` and sees nothing.

## Decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Sweep rule | One rule for every stored record: it stays if its author is allowlisted, or it holds a live pin, or it is the current manifest of an author who holds any live pin. Everything else is removed with its pin entry. Heads and manifests that point at a removed record go with it. This subsumes the expired pin and orphaned manifest passes |
| Delisting | No new mechanism. A reload sweeps, and the sweep rule drops the delisted author's unpinned records. Paid pins serve until they expire, honouring the receipt |
| Blob collection | `iroh-blobs` 0.103 keeps `delete` crate private but runs its own GC when the file store is opened with `GcConfig`. The relay opens the store with a protect callback that marks every blob a stored record references; anything else is collected at the GC interval. An index error aborts that run, never a collection |
| GC interval | `Relay::open` takes `every`, the interval for blob GC. The binary passes 60 s, tests pass milliseconds. `Swept.blobs` goes: the report has no consumer and GC owns blobs |
| Receipt | `weft push --pay` signs and verifies the receipt but stores it only after the relay reports it stored. A refused batch exits non zero with the relay's reasons and leaves nothing in the store |
| Dependencies | Nothing new |

## Layout

```
crates/net/src/index.rs          sweep with the one rule, blobs() for the protect set
crates/net/src/relay.rs          open(every) with GcConfig, has_blob for tests
crates/net/tests/relay.rs        delisting sweeps free records, blob GC collects a swept blob
crates/net/tests/pay.rs          pinned records and manifest survive a delisting
crates/relay/src/main.rs         every 60 s into open
crates/cli/src/main.rs           sign without storing, store the receipt after accept
crates/cli/src/net.rs            push returns the stored addresses
docs/relay.md, README.md         sweep and reload text, badges
```

## Plan

1. Index: rewrite `sweep` around the one rule; add `blobs()` scanning
   stored records for `Body::Blob`. Drop `Swept.blobs`.
2. Relay: `open(endpoint, dir, allow, pricing, every)` builds `Options`
   with `GcConfig { interval: every, add_protected }` over the index.
   `has_blob` for tests. Update the five call sites.
3. Tests: flip the delisting assertion in `relay.rs` and check the record
   is gone over the wire; a blob test where a live blob survives two GC
   intervals and a swept one is gone after two more; `pay.rs` checks a
   never listed author's pinned page and manifest survive a sweep that
   drops their unpinned pointer.
4. CLI: `pay` signs and verifies without storing; `push` returns stored
   addresses; `main` stores the receipt when it is among them, else fails.
5. Docs, README, smoke test the real binaries, close.

## Delivered

- One sweep rule in the index: a record stays if its author is
  allowlisted, it holds a live pin, or it is the current manifest of an
  author with a live pin. Delisting plus SIGHUP drops the free records.
- Blob collection: the relay opens its `iroh-blobs` store with `GcConfig`
  and a protect callback that marks every blob a stored record names. The
  interval is `Relay::open`'s `every`, 60 s in the binary. `Swept.blobs`
  is gone.
- `weft push --pay` stores the receipt only after the relay reports it
  stored; a refusal exits 1 with `receipt refused, nothing kept`.
- Tests: delisting sweeps and the record is gone over the wire, a
  referenced blob survives three GC intervals and a swept one is
  collected, pinned records and their manifest survive a sweep before the
  pin passes. 99 in the workspace.
- Smoke tested with the release binaries against a relay on the public
  iroh network: deny plus SIGHUP swept 5 records; a paid push to a relay
  with no bank exited 1 and `weft receipts` showed nothing; after `bank
  add`, `rate 1` and SIGHUP the same voucher was accepted and the receipt
  listed; the blob store shrank by the 200,000 byte blob after two GC
  intervals.

## Deviations from the plan

- None in substance. The blob GC test waits up to 25 intervals for the
  collector rather than exactly two, since `run_gc` sleeps before its
  first run and the relay's own writes race the clock.
